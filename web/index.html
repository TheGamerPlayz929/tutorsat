About this instrument

TutorSat

Practice sessions are assembled by two statistical models: a seeded Dirichlet–multinomial blueprint over the digital SAT framework, and a per-skill 2-parameter logistic IRT tracker that serves each next item at your Fisher-information peak.

Methodology, architecture, and design-system notes live in the repository's README.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, and DESIGN.md.
