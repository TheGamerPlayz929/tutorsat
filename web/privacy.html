Privacy policy

TutorSat

Last updated: 2026-08-23

What we collect

  • Your practice history — the questions you were served, your answers, correctness, and the statistical ability estimates derived from them.
  • Your profile basics — a display name, and, only if you choose Google Sign-In, your Google account identifier, name, and email address. These are used solely to recognize your account and keep your progress attached to it.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no trackers. This application does not embed third-party analytics scripts.

Where your data lives

  • Guest mode: entirely inside your own browser storage on your device. Clearing site data erases it.
  • Signed-in mode: in the application's own database on the server that hosts it, keyed to an anonymous id derived from your Google account identifier. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Google Sign-In

When you use Sign in with Google, Google receives the request to authenticate you and shows you the consent screen. TutorSat receives only your name, email address, and an immutable account identifier. TutorSat never sees your Google password. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings (Security > Third-party access).

Your controls

  • Export: the ACCOUNT panel lets you download all of your progress as a JSON file at any time.
  • Delete: deleting a profile removes its history; clearing your browser storage removes guest-mode history.
